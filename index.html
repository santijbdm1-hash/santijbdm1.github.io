My Journal

My Journal is a personal activity and life tracking app. It helps you record what you did each day, so over time you can look back and see how you actually spend your time. The aim is a quiet, organized place that feels like a notebook, not a dashboard demanding attention.

Main ideas

  • Log activities, routines, leisure and background activities.
  • Keep track of events and the people in your life.
  • Set goals and follow them over time.
  • Write reflections about each day.
  • Browse everything in a library of past entries.

My Wallet

My Wallet is my personal finance app. It focuses on keeping track of your money simply, so you can see what comes in, what goes out and what's left, without spreadsheets or clutter.

Main ideas

  • A dashboard with your finances at a glance.
  • Record income and expenses as transactions.
  • Budgets that show how much you have left to spend.
  • Overviews that explain where your money goes.
